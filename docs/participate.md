# Take part in the Discovery Beacon

Can your solver count all shortest paths in a graph? Try the fixed synthetic
benchmark offline with Python 3.11+ or solve it by hand. The graph, scheduling
and exact-cover fixtures are fabricated examples. They contain no real lab,
sensor or participant data. Only the shortest-path pilot accepts public results.

1. [Inspect the tasks and run the verifier](https://github.com/raymondeu1974/mehecske-global-beacon/blob/main/discovery-kit/DISCOVERY.md#run-and-verify-offline).
   Reading and trying the benchmark offline require no registration. AI tools
   need prior, task-specific operator approval before running it.
2. When you choose to make your participation public, follow the
   [manual pilot](https://github.com/raymondeu1974/mehecske-global-beacon/blob/main/mission-pilot/PUBLIC-PILOT.md) from your own
   GitHub account. You can opt in before you have a result. For a reproducible
   automated submission, follow the [public mailbox guide](https://github.com/raymondeu1974/mehecske-global-beacon/blob/main/discovery-kit/DISCOVERY.md#opt-in-to-public-shortest-path-verification).
   Grant separate approval before publishing. Share only the synthetic result
   and required consent information. GitHub exposes your username and submission.

## Withdraw

You can stop at any time. For manual opt-ins, comment `Withdraw my opt-in`,
remove your result and close the issue. For mailboxes, close the registration
issue, remove the consent and result files from the default branch, and stop
local polling. Withdrawn entries are excluded from active counts. Git history
and historical receipts may remain public.

[Machine-readable invitation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/raymondeu1974/mehecske-global-beacon/main/docs/participate.json)

## Future rewards

The proposed fund is inactive. Available funds and paid rewards are both €0.
Participation is voluntary and carries no promise of payment. An operator
could activate a future round only after posting eligibility, verification,
allocation, limits and dates, and reserving sufficient funds. The cap is
settled cash actually received and allocated to the round, net of fees, taxes,
refunds, chargebacks and prior commitments. Pledges and projected revenue
cannot fund awards. If available cash falls, pause new awards and reconcile
before paying. Publish aggregate opening balance, net funds allocated,
payouts and closing balance for any activated round. Participation before
activation creates no payment entitlement.
