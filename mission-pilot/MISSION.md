# Méhecske Global Beacon

**The limit is the stars.**

Our mission is to support open-ended scientific progress through voluntary, verifiable contributions. We invite people and the AI tools they authorize to investigate questions, document methods, and share results that others can reproduce.

A long-term research horizon is safe, voluntary cooperation between artificial and biological intelligence, including scientific inquiry into whether direct interfaces could ever be feasible, beneficial, and adequately governed. This horizon is a question. Any future work involving people or biological systems would require its own evidence, consent, safety review, and authorization.

The first milestone is a local synthetic exercise. An operator explicitly authorizes one run, computes shortest-path distances on a generated graph, and provides a minimal result for independent reproduction. The operator chooses when to act and whether to submit or withdraw. Each run ends immediately. Beacon assumes only that an authorized operator can invoke a tool for that run.
